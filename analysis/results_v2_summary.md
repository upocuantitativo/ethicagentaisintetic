orthogonal variables**, not two samples. Verification status split the 89 R0 rows
61/28; taxonomy label split the same 89 rows 81/8, because 20 rows carried a

κ = 0.772, bootstrap 95% CI **[0.543, 0.944]** (5,000 resamples), raw agreement 0.84,
PABAK 0.68, n = 25.

**The pre-registered threshold κ ≥ 0.70 is NOT established**: the CI lower bound is

Per-class specific agreement: Dismissed 1.00, Agentic DAO 0.909, Agentic

**The R0 manuscript mis-describes the disagreements.** It states that three fell on
the Synthetic Company vs AI-Enhanced Shell frontier and one on Multi-Agent System vs
Agentic DAO. The rater records show:

| Entity | Rater 1 | Rater 2 |

No disagreement falls on the Synthetic/Shell frontier. All four involve the

remain in the analytic sample** (κ = 0.846, CI [0.459, 1.000] — uninformative at that
n). Reliability must therefore be reported as a limitation, not a strength.

## 4. Synthetic score as a detector only (comments 1, 4)

The score is removed from every primary model and evaluated only as a detector.

| Evaluation set | n | AUC | 95% CI |
|---|---|---|---|
| All observed entities | 45 | 0.921 | [0.830, 0.985] |
| **Stage-4 rows, score played no selection role** | **29** | **0.900** | **[0.783, 1.000]** |

At the 0.50 cut-off on the score-independent subsample the detector's operating
point is reported in `results_v2.json`. Caveat that must be stated in the paper: for
Stage-4 rows the score was assigned by the analyst applying the same rubric rather
than computed by the automated pipeline, so this establishes independence of
*selection*, not independence of *analyst judgement*. 15 Stage-4 rows carry the
loader's default value of exactly 50 and are flagged in the audit dataset.

## 5. Supervised classification (comments 1, 5, 8)

Target = verification status (verified synthetic/agentic vs control), which is what
P1 actually claims — not the R0 "synthetic-or-DAO vs agentic-or-other" collapse.
All preprocessing (median imputation, scaling, one-hot encoding) inside folds.
Nested CV: inner 3-fold grid search, outer 10×5 repeated stratified.
Baselines: majority 0.578, stratified 0.511. Permutation test: 300 permutations.

| Specification | Estimator | Accuracy | 95% CI | Balanced acc. | F1-macro | AUC | perm. p |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary** (no score, no detector variable) | Logistic | 0.822 | [0.581, 1.000] | 0.811 | 0.808 | 0.868 | 0.003 |
| **Primary** | Random forest | **0.858** | [0.667, 1.000] | 0.842 | 0.844 | 0.911 | 0.003 |
| Sensitivity (+ AI-text detector score) | Logistic | 0.813 | [0.581, 1.000] | 0.797 | 0.794 | 0.851 | 0.003 |

**Answer to "show how performance changes" (comment 1): removing the score costs
about 7.5 accuracy points (0.933 → 0.858).** The defensible headline is 0.86, not
the R0 figure of 0.92–0.94.

Adding the AI-text detector variable *lowers* performance in both estimators,
supporting its demotion to a sensitivity analysis.

Out-of-fold confusion matrix, primary random forest (rows true, cols predicted;
0 = control, 1 = verified case): `[[24, 2], [5, 14]]`. Per class — control:
precision 0.828, recall 0.923; verified case: precision 0.875, recall 0.737.

Group-aware CV by source family (4 groups): accuracy 0.848 (SD 0.092).

## 7. Clustering (comment 7)

Full matrix: 3 algorithms (k-means, agglomerative, Gaussian mixture) × k ∈ {3,4,5} ×
{with score, without score} = 18 configurations. **All variables standardised before
clustering** (z-scores), which the R0 manuscript failed to state.

Ex-ante criterion declared before inspecting the ARIs: partial recovery = ARI in
**[0.15, 0.45]**; below 0.15 the taxonomy is unmoored from the data's dominant
variance; above 0.45 it is a re-description of it.

Result: ARI vs taxonomy **median 0.090, range [0.000, 0.216]**; only **11.1%** of
configurations fall inside the pre-set band.

**P3 is therefore NOT supported on the reconstructed sample.** The unsupervised
structure does not recover the analyst coding even partially by the stated
criterion. This must be reported as a disconfirmed proposition.

## 8. Missingness (comment 8)

AI-text detector score is missing for **30 of 45 (66.7%)**. Missingness is perfectly
predicted by recruitment channel (Stage-4: 29/29 missing; Stage-3: 1/16 missing;
χ² p < 10⁻⁵). Association with verification status is not significant on its own
(Fisher p = 0.116), but the channel confound means the data are **not missing at
random**, and median imputation can bias in either direction. The primary model
therefore excludes the variable entirely.
